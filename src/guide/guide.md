## Before you start

Talk with your GM about the game, and agree on any limits for the
characters: see [Discussion](page:DiscussionPage) and
[Limiting the Character](page:LimitsPage). Set them with
**Character > Limits...**.

## The Abilities

Choose an Ability's name under the list, such as [](item:Attack), set
its level and its detail, and press **Add**.
[Show me](show:abilities)

- **?** opens the rulebook at the chosen name's page.
- Click a line to change it, then press **Change** or **Remove**.
